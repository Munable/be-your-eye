Early products · 开发与小范围验证中
01 / MOBILE WEB

Superbrowser

手机网页助手

Help with the next tap.

An Android browser assistant that uses page screenshots to guide taps, text input, and navigation on your phone. You can take over as a task progresses.

根据页面截图判断下一步，在手机上执行点击、输入和浏览操作。任务进行时，你可以随时接管。

In development and device testing
开发与设备验证中
02 / VISUAL MONITORING

Be Your Eye

帮你盯

Keep an eye on a fixed scene.

Use a phone camera to watch for a configured visual target or condition. Visual processing runs on the phone, with cloud services supporting account, sync, and assistant features.

用手机摄像头持续关注固定画面中的指定目标或条件。视觉分析在本机完成，账户、同步及助手功能使用相应云服务。

In internal validation
内部验证中，尚未正式发行

03 / FOOD JOURNAL

Nutrition Town

营养小镇

A picture of your everyday meals.

A web app for meal-photo records, background analysis, nutrition trends, and reviewing or correcting entries. Nutrition values are estimates for personal reference.

拍照记录饮食，后台分析与保存，查看营养趋势并查询、纠正记录。营养数据为估算值，供日常参考。
